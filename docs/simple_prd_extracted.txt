1 What is BuildTrack?
BuildTrack is an app that lets people who are paying for a building project see, check and approve the
work remotely — and gives the construction company one place to run the project.
In one sentence
You only count work as "done" when you've seen the proof and approved it — and every payment,
message and decision is kept on record.
2 The problem
People can't see what's really happening on site. A Nigerian living abroad who is building a house back
home usually relies on a few WhatsApp photos and trust.
Money goes missing or gets argued about. There's no clear record of what was paid, for which part of the
work, or whether it was received.
WhatsApp isn't a record. Chats get deleted, phones get lost, staﬀ leave — and the history goes with them.
Good builders can't prove they're good. Honest companies have no easy way to show a track record to
new clients.
Existing software doesn't fit. Big tools like Procore are expensive, complicated and built for large
American companies, not for Nigerian builders and their clients.

4 How it works, step by step
1. Get verified
The construction company signs up
and BuildTrack checks it's a real,
registered company.
→
2. Connect
The client picks a company from
the list (or invites their own), or the

→
3. Make the plan
The company sets out the stages
(foundation, roofing…) and when

4. Agree the plan
The client reviews it and accepts.
The plan is then locked; any change
must be agreed by both sides.
→
5. Build & prove
The crew takes photos and videos
in the app. Each one is stamped
with the time and location.
→
6. Approve
When a stage is finished, the client
checks the proof and approves it —
or sends it back with reasons.
7. Pay
Approving a stage makes its payment due. The client pays
by bank transfer and uploads the receipt; the company
confirms it.
→
8. Finish & rate
When the build is done, the client rates the company and
the company rates the client.
5 Main features
Feature In simple terms
Verified companies only Companies must pass checks (CAC registration, tax number, director's ID, bank
account in the company's name) before they can use the app.
Stage templates Ready-made lists of stages for every type of project — bungalows, duplexes,
commercial buildings, roads, bridges and more.
Proof you can trust Photos and videos taken in the app carry the time and location. Each stage needs a
minimum amount of proof before it can be sent for approval.
Real progress The progress percentage only counts stages the client has approved — not just what
the builder says.
Payment records What's due, what's paid, receipts and confirmations — all in one place.
Built-in chat WhatsApp-style chat inside the app for the company's staﬀ and its clients, so
conversations stay with the project.
Notifications Phone alerts for important things (mostly app notifications, email when action is
needed, SMS only for urgent things).

6 How money and disagreements are handled
In the first version, money does not go through the app. People pay by normal bank transfer, and the app
keeps the record.
Paying: when a payment is due, the client sees the company's bank details, pays, then uploads the receipt
with the transaction reference.
Confirming: the company has 3 days to confirm it received the money, or dispute it. If it says nothing, the
payment is marked "Paid – not confirmed by contractor".
Protection against fraud: only the company's Director can change its bank account, the account name
must match the registered company name, clients are alerted immediately, and payments pause for 48
hours after any change.
Disagreements: either side can open a dispute about a payment or a piece of work. The project pauses,
both sides add evidence, and both must agree it's resolved. If it isn't sorted within 14 days, it shows as
"Unresolved dispute" on both profiles.
Slow clients: if a client doesn't review a stage, they get reminders. After 7 days it's marked overdue and the
company can carry on — the delay is shown as the client's.
7 What makes BuildTrack diﬀerent
Built for the client, not just the builder — clients have real power to approve work.
Progress is proven, not just reported.
Payments are tied to approved work and every step is on record.
Only genuine, checked companies — and clients verify their identity too, so nobody can escape a bad
reputation.
Made for Nigeria — naira by default, works on low-cost Android phones and poor network, uses local
terms (DPC, lintel, decking).

8 How BuildTrack makes money
Who Pays?
Construction companies Yes — a monthly or yearly subscription based on how many projects they're
running at once. Their first project is free until it's finished (up to 12 months).
Unlimited staﬀ.
Individual clients Free, always.
Governments & organisations Free during "Early Access" at launch; a paid plan comes later.
Actual prices will be decided later.
9 What comes when
Release What's included
First launch Everything described above: verification, projects, stage templates, proof, approvals, payment
records, disputes, chat, notifications, ratings, share links, worker rewards, web + mobile apps.
Soon after Pausing or cancelling a project, switching to a new company mid-build, tracking expenses and
materials.
Version 2 Paying directly through the app, BuildTrack helping settle disputes, paid plans for organisations,
more than one company per project.
Later Money held safely until work is approved (escrow), independent site inspections, camera and
drone support.
10 Still to decide
