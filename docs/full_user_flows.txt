=== Page 2 ===
CO-1 Website: sign-up and the two front doors Everyone new · public visitors COMPANY SIDE
The public website does three jobs: show the directory of approved companies, let new people sign up, and send returning people to the right login. Clients and companies enter
through diﬀerent doors.
Rules
The public directory is view-only. "Connect" always leads to sign-up; nothing is sent
from a public page.
Returning clients have one login page. The role comes from the account, so there's
no picker.
The "Company login" link is small and away from sign-up, so the public never
mistakes it for a way in.
WB-04 Sign up: content
Two large cards: I'm a client (building or overseeing a project) and I'm a
construction company
One line under the company card: "Companies are checked and approved by
Vantaged before they get access"
=== Page 3 ===
CO-2 Company application and approval Company applicant · Vantaged admin · Director
COMPANY SIDE
A company ﬁlls in an application, which creates no account. After the admin approves it, the admin creates the
Director's login (the Director's email plus a generated password) and Vantaged emails the company login link with
those details. The Director is not forced to change the password. They're told it was generated by Vantaged's system,
and a change-password prompt sits in their notiﬁcations.

=== Page 4 ===
CO-3 Company login with role selection All company staﬀ COMPANY SIDE
Staﬀ reach this page from the login link in their SMS or email, or from the small "Company login" link on the website. They
pick their role, then enter their details. A login only works for the role(s) the Director gave it.
CO-L01 ﬁelds
Role ﬁrst: Director · Site Manager · Oﬃce Coordinator ·
Crew Member (4 tappable cards)
Email or phone (+234 default) · password with
show/hide
"Remember my role on this device"
Rules
The error never says which part was wrong (role or
password)
Wrong-role attempts count toward the lock and appear
in the Director's team log
To switch role: log out, then log in with the other role
Staﬀ set their own password on ﬁrst login. The Director
is only prompted (CO-2).
=== Page 5 ===
CO-4 Director creates staﬀ logins Director only COMPANY SIDE
Staﬀ can't sign themselves up. Right after approval, the Director adds the whole team at once (CO-T04). New hires later are added one at a time (CO-T02). Each login is tied to the
role the Director chose.
CO-T04 Add many at once
Same ﬁelds as CO-T02, as table rows, or a CSV/Excel upload with a downloadable
template
Problems shown per row before sending (duplicate phone, missing role)
Work days per person (for reward check-ins, if rewards are on)
CO-T01 Team list
Name · role(s) · projects · status (Login sent / Active / Suspended / Removed) · last
active
Actions: edit access, reset password, resend login, suspend, remove
Activity log: logins, failed and wrong-role attempts, role changes

CO-5 Project requests, creating a project and agreeing the plan
Director, Site Manager, Oﬃce Coordinator create · Director or Site Manager send COMPANY SIDE
Only companies create projects. A project starts from a client's project request, or the company creates it directly and
invites its own client (or runs it with no client connected).

CO-7 Crew Member: check in, capture proof, ﬁnish tasks Crew Member · works oﬄine
COMPANY SIDE
The crew app is basically one screen with a big camera button. Everything works without signal and uploads later with the
original time and place. Crew use the team chat only, never the client chat.
Gallery photos: allowed, but labelled "Not captured in app" and they don't count toward minimum proof. Show that
label in CO-C04 and on the stage page.

=== Page 11 ===
CL-1 Client sign-up: Client Admin or Client Rep New clients CLIENT SIDE
Individual Client Admins sign up and start at once, with no approval and no ID check yet. Organisations (governments,
companies) apply and wait for approval, like construction companies. Client Reps can only join with a link code from
their Client Admin, so no Rep can exist without an Admin.

Allowed before the ID check
Browse Find companies, view proﬁles and ratings
Open share links they've been sent
Set up their proﬁle
Needs the ID check
Sending or accepting project requests and invites
Accepting plans, approving stages, recording payments
Rating, and chatting with companies

=== Page 13 ===
CL-3 Client Admin invites and manages Client Reps Client Admin CLIENT SIDE
One button sends the invite. It was designed for clients who aren't technical.
Rep permissions (chosen per rep)
View progress · approve/reject stages · record payments · suggest changes · message the company · manage share links · invite people. Accepting the plan and change
requests is Client Admin only, whatever a Rep's permissions. Reps can view and comment.
=== Page 14 ===
CL-4 Finding a company and sending a project request
Client Admin (ID-veriﬁed to send) CLIENT SIDE
Clients never create projects. They send a short project request (a brief), and the company creates the project. A client
can have up to 3 open requests for the same job. In v1 each project has one company.
